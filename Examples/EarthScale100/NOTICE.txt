The player Earth's geographic water reference uses the Ocean and Lakes polygons
from Natural Earth v5.1.2 at 1:10 million scale, in the public domain. The global
marine polygon supplies tidal-water connectivity. The disconnected inland
polygon and mapped lakes supply lake outlines. Reference levels are estimated
from shoreline elevations on the project's ETOPO overview. Narrow straits may
provide connectivity beneath exposed coarse cells. The generator represents
area-averaged terrain and cannot resolve every coastline or seasonal lake.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson

python Tools/EarthScale100/bake_player_water.py --check reconstructs the five
EarthWater resources from SHA-256-verified source geometry using NumPy and
Pillow. Raw geometry downloads are cached under ValidationOutput/BasinWater.

The 100 m grid is derived from the 1 arc-second GLO-30 digital surface model and the 15 arc-second ETOPO surface elevation and seafloor grid. Heights are rounded to whole real metres relative to EGM2008. The metadata lists the exact input files and SHA-256 checksums. Cropping, interpolation, projection and combination were performed by the ORBIS test-map generator.
