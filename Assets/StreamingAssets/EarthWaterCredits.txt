ORBIS Earth preset water data

Lake Dianchi, Lake Erhai, Lake Fuxian and Lake Sayram use HydroLAKES v1.0.
Messager, M. L., Lehner, B., Grill, G., Nedeva, I. and Schmitt, O. (2016).
Estimating the volume and age of water stored in global lakes using a
geo-statistical approach. Nature Communications 7, 13603.
https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13603
https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrolakes

HydroLAKES is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
ORBIS selects four polygons, rounds their geographic coordinates, rasterizes
their shores, and uses the source reference elevations on the simulation grid.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

River widths and portions of surveyed river and estuary centre lines use Global River
Widths from Landsat (GRWL) by George H. Allen and Tamlin M. Pavelsky, summary
statistics V01.02 and vector V01.01, licensed under CC BY 4.0. ORBIS matches
and smooths Landsat transects and reach medians, interpolates widths, and
registers selected courses to ordered transect centres. Junctions and tidal
outlets are tied to the mapped network and shorelines.
https://zenodo.org/records/18624542
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat0636
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Other geographic river and lake outlines and ocean shores use Natural Earth
v5.1.2, public domain. The Yangtze estuary chart line is derived from its banks.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Nile Delta cropland uses ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200, Zanaga et al. (2022).
© ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data
(2021) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium. ORBIS clips cropland to the
Natural Earth delta and aggregates it to geographic crop fractions.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7254221
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
