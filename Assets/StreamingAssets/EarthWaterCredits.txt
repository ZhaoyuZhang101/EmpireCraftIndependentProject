ORBIS Earth preset water data

Lake Dianchi, Lake Erhai, Lake Fuxian and Lake Sayram use HydroLAKES v1.0.
Messager, M. L., Lehner, B., Grill, G., Nedeva, I. and Schmitt, O. (2016).
Estimating the volume and age of water stored in global lakes using a
geo-statistical approach. Nature Communications 7, 13603.
https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13603
https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrolakes

HydroLAKES is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
ORBIS selects four polygons, rounds their geographic coordinates, rasterizes
their shores, and uses the source reference elevations on the simulation grid.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Other geographic river and lake outlines and ocean shores use Natural Earth
v5.1.2, public domain. The Yangtze estuary chart line is derived from its banks.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
